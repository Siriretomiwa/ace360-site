**1. Short title:** 4 questions to ask before you pay a web designer
**Series:** Quick explainers (8)

**2. Educational objective:** Teach small-business owners the four things to settle before a website project starts:
(1) an itemised scope with a total, (2) seeing the design before the build, (3) owning the domain, hosting and logins,
(4) who looks after the site after launch. Useful whoever they hire.

**3. Target duration:** 54.5 seconds (1080 × 1920, 9:16)

**4. Hook (0–3.4 s):** "Before you pay a web designer, ask these four questions." Four numbered circles pop in.

## 5. Full voiceover (≈130 words · voice **am_michael**, US male, calm)
**Copy-paste version** (`reels/s02-four-questions/voiceover-paste.txt`, only needed for a manual recording):

```
Before you pay a web designer, ask these four questions.
<break time="1.0s" />
Most website problems start before anything is built: a vague quote, a late design, or logins you never get.
<break time="1.0s" />
Question one: what's included, and what's the total? A quote that just says "website" leaves room for surprises. Ask for every item on its own line.
<break time="1.0s" />
Question two: will I see the design before you build it? Changing a sketch takes minutes. Changing a finished site can take days.
<break time="1.0s" />
Question three: who owns the domain, the hosting and the logins? They should be in your name, so you're never locked out.
<break time="1.0s" />
Question four: what happens after launch? Ask who installs the updates, who makes the backups, and who you call when something breaks.
<break time="1.0s" />
Get all four answers in writing, before you pay. Ask them on any first call, including ours. Follow for more website tips.
```
