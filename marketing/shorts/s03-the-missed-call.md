Role: second relaunch Short for YouTube, the first in the new **storyteller** style: slower voice, a short pause after every
sentence, a story with a beginning, a turn and an ending. Ends on like / share / subscribe.

**1. Short title:** She missed one call. Her website could have answered it.
**Series:** Story (a new format: one made-up small business, one problem, three fixes). Related: "If I built a website for…"

**2. Educational objective:** Show service businesses (salons, barbers, therapists, trades) that a website can answer
the questions people phone about: (1) show prices, even as "from" prices, (2) let people book online with real free times,
(3) send automatic reminders. The story makes the cost of a missed call concrete without inventing any statistics.

**3. Target duration:** 59.5 seconds (1080 × 1920, 9:16)

**4. Hook (0–5.4 s):** "Tuesday, eleven in the morning. Noor is halfway through a haircut, when her phone rings."
On screen: a phone ringing and shaking, with orange ring waves, and "*Tuesday*, 11:00."

## 5. Full voiceover (≈140 words · voice **af_heart** · speed 0.9 · 0.35 s pause after each sentence)
**Copy-paste version** (`reels/s03-the-missed-call/voiceover-paste.txt`, only needed for a manual recording; read it slowly,
like telling a story to one person):

```
Tuesday, eleven in the morning. Noor is halfway through a haircut, when her phone rings.
<break time="1.0s" />
She can't answer. The caller doesn't leave a message. She just books somewhere else.
<break time="1.0s" />
That night, Noor opens her own website, and sees what the caller saw. A phone number. And "call us for prices".
<break time="1.0s" />
So she changes three things. One: her prices, out in the open. A "from" price answers what people were calling to ask.
<break time="1.0s" />
Two: a booking button, with her real free times. Now someone can book at midnight, without calling.
<break time="1.0s" />
Three: a reminder, sent automatically the day before, so appointments are harder to forget.
<break time="1.0s" />
Next Tuesday, eleven o'clock. The phone stays quiet. The new bookings came in overnight.
<break time="1.0s" />
Noor is made up. The missed call isn't. When your hands are busy, let your website answer.
<break time="1.0s" />
If this was useful, give it a like, share it with someone who runs a salon, and subscribe for more.
```
