# Maple Lane Stories: channel plan

**What it is:** short Roblox story mini-movies about a group of friends at Maple Lane Middle School.
Friendship, school drama, small wins. Every episode is a complete story with a twist or payoff,
so viewers get the full arc in 2–4 minutes, plus a "next time" teaser.

**Why this format:** story videos keep people watching (a story pulls you to the ending), every
episode is genuinely different (which is what YouTube's monetization review looks for), and it's
filmed inside real Roblox, so there's no copyright problem with Roblox's avatars.

## Identity
- **Name:** Maple Lane Stories (backups: Maple Lane Life, The Maple Lane Crew)
- **Handle:** @MapleLaneStories (check it's free when you create the channel)
- **Tagline:** "New story every week on Maple Lane."
- **Look:** Fredoka One font, purple (#3A1C66) and yellow (#FFE45C), bright daylight sets.
- **Main cast:** Mia (the quiet artist), Jay (kind, a bit goofy), Zoe (popular, learning to be nicer),
  Kai (the class clown), Principal Grant, plus Sam and Lily in the background.

## Posting rhythm
- **1 episode a week**, same day and time (for example, Saturday 10:00).
- **2 Shorts per episode**, posted mid-week, each ending on "full story on the channel".
- After 6–8 episodes, compile them into a 15–20 minute "Season 1" video. Longer videos earn more.

## Season 1 episode ideas
1. **The New Girl Who Never Talked**: Mia's mural wins the contest. *(script written, ready to record)*
2. **Mia's First Sleepover**: Zoe invites Mia; Mia is scared she'll be left out.
3. **Kai's Fake Excuse**: Kai pretends to be sick to skip the talent show. Why?
4. **The Lost Sketchbook**: Mia's sketchbook goes missing the day before an art fair.
5. **Zoe Gets It Wrong**: a rumour Zoe started blows up, and she has to fix it.
6. **Jay's Secret Job**: Jay keeps leaving early. The crew follows him.
7. **The Principal's Challenge**: the whole school against the teachers in an obby race.
8. **Season Finale: Maple Lane Festival**: everyone's storylines meet.

## Passing the monetization review
- Every episode a new story with real dialogue and a payoff. No near-identical uploads.
- Title and thumbnail must match what happens in the video (no fake clickbait).
- Wholesome and safe for younger viewers: no violence, romance or scary content.
- If you mention Roblox in titles or descriptions, keep the line "Not affiliated with Roblox."
- **Audience setting:** answer YouTube's "Is it made for kids?" question honestly for each video.
  If the stories are mainly aimed at under-13s, YouTube's rules (COPPA) say choose **Yes**.
  Aiming the stories at roughly 11–15 and choosing "No" is only right if that's genuinely the main audience.
