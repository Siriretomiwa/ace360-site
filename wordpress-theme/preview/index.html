How we work

From first callto launchin 8 weeks.

Typical timeline for a brand website. Stores and 3D work take a little longer; we tell you up front.

  1. 01

    Kennismaken · Week 1

    Intro call

    Thirty minutes about your brand, your customers and what the site has to achieve. You get a written plan and a fixed quote within a week.

  2. 02

    Ontwerpen · Weeks 2–3

    Strategy & design

    Sitemap, content plan and a clickable design of the key pages. Motion is designed here too, so nothing is bolted on later.

  3. 03

    Bouwen · Weeks 4–7

    Build

    We build on a staging site you can follow along on. Payments, translations and integrations are tested with real orders.

  4. 04

    Lanceren · Week 8

    Launch

    Redirects, search console, speed and accessibility checks, then go-live. Your team gets a short training on editing the site.

  5. 05

    Groeien · Ongoing

    Care & growth

    Updates, backups and a monthly report. We propose improvements based on what visitors actually do.

Built for the Netherlands

Made for howthe Dutch buy.

  • iDEAL, Bancontact, Klarna

    Checkout through Mollie, with the payment methods Dutch and Belgian customers expect.

  • Postcode + huisnummer

    Address autofill from postcode and house number. Fewer typos, fewer failed deliveries.

  • AVG-proof

    Cookie consent that blocks trackers until visitors agree. Fonts and scripts served from your own server.

  • European Accessibility Act

    Built to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation, contrast, screen readers and reduced motion.

  • Nederlands & English

    Bilingual content with proper hreflang, so both versions rank in search.

  • PostNL, DHL, Sendcloud

    Shipping labels and track-and-trace emails straight from your WooCommerce orders.
