The problem

Most sites are slow, generic and hard to change.

  • Six seconds before anything appears on a phone
  • No iDEAL, so Dutch carts get abandoned
  • A template that looks like everyone else
  • Nobody on the team dares to edit it

What we believe

Your website is the hardest-working employee your brand has. It should look the part.

  1. Fast first. Every effect earns its weight in kilobytes.
  2. Dutch by design. iDEAL, postcodes, AVG and two languages from day one.
  3. Yours to edit. Your team changes any page without calling us.
  4. Motion with a reason. 3D where it explains, stillness where it doesn’t.

How we build

Five steps. Eight weeks.

The typical timeline for a brand website. Stores and 3D work take a little longer, and we tell you that up front.

01 / 05 · Kennismaken · Week 1

Listen

Thirty minutes about your brand, your customers and what the site has to achieve. You get a sitemap and a fixed quote within a week.

02 / 05 · Ontwerpen · Weeks 2–3

Design

A clickable design of the key pages, with the motion designed in from the start. You approve it before we write a line of code.

03 / 05 · Bouwen · Weeks 4–7

Build

Layer by layer on a staging site you can follow. Payments, translations and integrations are tested with real orders.

04 / 05 · Lanceren · Week 8

Launch

Redirects, speed and accessibility checks, then go-live. Your team gets a short training on editing every page.

05 / 05 · Groeien · Ongoing

Grow

Updates, backups and a monthly report on speed, search and sales, with concrete proposals for what to improve next.

Built for the Netherlands · 1 / 6

  1. iDEAL checkout

    iDEAL, Bancontact and Klarna through Mollie. The payment methods Dutch and Belgian customers expect, in a checkout that takes one screen.

  2. Postcode autofill

    Postcode plus house number fills in street and city. Fewer typos, fewer parcels returned to sender.

  3. AVG-proof

    A cookie banner that actually blocks trackers until visitors agree. Fonts and scripts come from your own server.

  4. Accessible

    Built to WCAG 2.2 AA for the European Accessibility Act: keyboard navigation, contrast and screen-reader labels.

  5. Nederlands & English

    Both languages with proper hreflang, so each version ranks in search on its own.

  6. PostNL, DHL, Sendcloud

    Shipping labels and track-and-trace emails straight from your WooCommerce orders.
