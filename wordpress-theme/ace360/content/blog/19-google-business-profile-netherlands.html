When someone in Amsterdam searches “hair salon near me” or “plumber Rotterdam”, Google usually shows a map with three businesses before any website. Those come from Google Business Profiles. A profile is free, and for any business with local customers it is one of the most useful things to set up.

Step 1: create the profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in with a Google account for your business.
  2. Enter your business name exactly as you use it everywhere else. Do not add keywords or city names to it; that breaks Google’s rules.
  3. Pick the main category that best describes you, such as “Hair salon” or “Plumber”.

Step 2: address or service area

If customers come to you (shop, salon, practice), add your address. If you go to customers or work from home, set a service area instead: the towns or regions you cover. Your home address then stays hidden.

Step 3: verify

Google needs to confirm your business is real. Depending on your situation you verify by video, phone, text message or a postcard with a code. Your profile is not visible until verification is done.

Step 4: fill it in for your customers

  • Opening hours, including Dutch public holidays such as King’s Day.
  • Phone number and website, linking to the most relevant page.
  • Services or products, with short descriptions and “from” prices where possible.
  • Description: what you do, for whom and where. If you serve both Dutch and English speakers, mention that you work in English.
  • Photos: logo, cover, your place inside and out, your work, your team. Real photos, not stock.
  • Booking link, if customers can book online.

Which language?

Your profile has one main description. Write it in the language most of your customers use. If they are mostly Dutch, write it in Dutch and add a sentence in English, for example “We also work in English.” Google can show automatic translations to people who search in another language.

Step 5: reviews

Ask every happy customer for a review; the profile gives you a direct link to share. Reply to every review, including critical ones, calmly and specifically. Never buy or trade reviews.

Step 6: keep it current

Update holiday hours, add new photos now and then, answer questions people ask on your profile, and check the statistics: how many calls, route requests and website visits it brings.

Profile and website work together

The profile sends people to your site, and your site confirms they came to the right place. Keep your name, address and phone number identical on both. More in our guide to SEO in the Netherlands.
