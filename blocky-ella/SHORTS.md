# Goodnight, Ella: YouTube Shorts

Vertical 1080×1920, about 27 seconds each, built to loop.

## Upload settings (both)
- Audience: **Yes, it's made for kids** (required for toddler content).
- Do not put "Roblox" in the title, tags or thumbnail. The avatar is original, so say "blocky" instead.
- Add both Shorts to a "Goodnight, Ella" playlist and link the full song as the Short's related video.
